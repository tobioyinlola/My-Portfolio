## Publishable article

It’s Thursday afternoon. Sales has promised a Friday release, Engineering has found an intermittent failure, and Support has yet to see the workflow. People can agree that the release matters and still disagree on what “ready” means.

I’d ask what we’re exposing, to whom, and what happens if it fails. In a candidate invitation workflow, a formatting defect may be manageable for a small cohort. Duplicate invitations after a retry call for evidence that sends are safe or a release that keeps sending under manual control.

Compare full launch, draft-only access, and holding the release. For each option, document the user value, the risk, and the recovery plan. Ask the release owner to decide and assign each follow-up.

A draft-only pilot can still test whether recruiters find the invitation workflow useful, while giving Engineering time to validate delivery. Sales can explain what the pilot includes, and Support gets a clear escalation path.

## Team playbook

1. Product defines the release cohort, decision owner, and criteria for full release, limited release, or hold. Share the open issues and options before the meeting.

2. Engineering, QA, and Support present evidence on the highest-impact failures, rollback controls, training, and incident response. For the invitation example, test retry behavior and verify whether a send was recorded before another attempt can proceed.

3. The release owner records the decision and signs off on the scope. Assign an on-call owner, a stop trigger, a communication owner, and a date for reconsideration.

| Decision item | Worked example |
| --- | --- |
| Go condition | Full release requires verified retry behavior, support readiness, and named incident ownership. Draft-only access can proceed for the agreed cohort once tested. |
| Stop and recover | If duplicate sends occur, disable automated sending. Review impacted invitations and coordinate any candidate communication with the operations owner. |
| Reconsideration | Engineering retests retry behavior. Support confirms readiness. The release owner reviews the evidence before expanding access. |
