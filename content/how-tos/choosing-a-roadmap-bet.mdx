## Publishable article

Imagine your team has one delivery slot. Sales wants an automated report to unblock a prospect. Customer Success wants a faster onboarding flow. Engineering wants time to fix an integration that keeps breaking. Each request makes sense on its own. The roadmap decision starts when you ask what taking one on would delay.

If the quarterly goal is faster onboarding, I’d examine where setups stall. If integration failures prevent completion, the onboarding and reliability requests may belong to one bet. If the setup screen causes most delays, the team has a different problem to solve.

For Sales, I’d check whether reporting is a signed commitment. A manual report may cover the immediate need while we verify demand from other customers.

A score such as RICE, which stands for reach, impact, confidence, and effort, can make assumptions visible. The final call still needs a short explanation of the outcome, the evidence, what gets deferred, and what would reopen the decision.

## Team playbook

1. Product writes the outcome and the decision deadline. Ask Sales, Customer Success, and Engineering to provide one evidence-backed case each.

2. Product and Engineering compare reach in the same time window, impact on the chosen outcome, evidence strength, effort, dependencies, and work displaced. Separate mandatory obligations from discretionary bets.

3. The decision owner chooses a bet, gives deferred work a revisit condition, and sends the written decision to stakeholders.

| Capture this | Worked example |
| --- | --- |
| Decision and trade-off | Improve onboarding reliability this cycle. Defer automated reporting and offer a manual report during prospect discovery. |
| Evidence and uncertainty | Review stalled setups and integration incidents. Sales verifies whether reporting is a buying condition. |
| Review trigger | Reopen the reporting decision when evidence shows repeat demand or a material change to the commercial commitment. |

## Practice exercise

You have two engineer-weeks. Twelve of 40 recent setups stalled, and nine of those stalls involved the same integration. Sales has one unsigned prospect asking for automated reports. Choose this cycle’s bet and name the evidence that would change your choice.
