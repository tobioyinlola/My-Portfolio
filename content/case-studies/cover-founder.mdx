Cover Founder turns a founder's announcement into a broadcast-style news video, a press release and a story ready for distribution.

[Visit Cover Founder](https://coverfounder.com)

## Context and problem

Startups launch products, raise money and hire, but much of that news never gets covered. Media coverage is often slow, expensive and geared toward larger companies. Founders need a way to turn a real announcement into a story a newsroom can use, without managing separate interview, writing, video and distribution workflows.

## Who it's for

Founders with an announcement to share: a product launch, funding round, hiring milestone or other company news. Cover Founder brings the interview, story production, approval and distribution choices into one browser-based flow.

## Discovery and key insight

The product connects story creation with distribution. A founder can tell the story in their own words, review the resulting release, and choose where to send it from the same platform.

## Options considered and decisions

The workflow makes several product decisions visible:

- **A four-question interview:** an AI presenter guides the founder through short answers rather than asking for a finished press release.
- **Recording checks before production:** recording begins just before the countdown ends to avoid losing the first words. Answers that are too dark or too short are re-recorded.
- **Approval before charging:** the founder reviews the story before committing to production.
- **Outlet-level purchasing:** filters, a shortlist and a running credit total let the founder choose distribution and track each outlet separately.

## AI approach

LLMs generate interview questions and write a wire-style release from the founder's own answers. The platform flags claims it cannot verify and keeps founder approval in the loop.

LiveKit supports the interview agent. HeyGen supplies the AI presenters, Marcus Richard and Amara Dickson, both presented as Business Reporters for CF News. Remotion renders the broadcast-style video with an opening, questions, founder answers, name cards, headlines, a live badge and a ticker.

A versioned production rulebook defines the video format. Saved reference videos provide a baseline for checking whether a change alters the output unexpectedly.

## What I built

The product includes:

- **Interview flow:** announcement selection, browser recording, countdowns and automatic answer checks.
- **Story and press release:** writing from the founder's own words, claim flags and approval before charging.
- **Video production:** presenter segments and founder answers assembled into a consistent broadcast format, with a queue to keep production fair under load.
- **Publishing:** a public page for each story, with sharing and downloads.
- **Credit wallet:** card payments, local bank transfer, refunds and referrals recorded in a full ledger.
- **Distribution catalogue:** outlet pricing, logos and filters for topic, region and country, with a shortlist and per-outlet tracking.

The distribution catalogue spans Africa, North America, the Middle East and Europe. Named outlets include Bloomberg, Fortune, Reuters, the Toronto Star, Forbes Middle East, Gulf News, Nairametrics and Techpoint Africa. Catalogue availability is separate from a confirmed placement.
